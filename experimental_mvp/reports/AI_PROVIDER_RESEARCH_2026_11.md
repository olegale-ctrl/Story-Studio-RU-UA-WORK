## Verification limits

Dynamic pages can omit plan badges or require login. Maximum resolution, model-specific duration/cost, download rules, Google login and automation terms remain UNKNOWN unless explicitly recorded. Homepage feature marketing is not evidence of free-tier eligibility. API existence never authorizes scraping a website, reusing browser cookies or spending free web credits through an API. Login happens only in the system browser. No CAPTCHA/MFA or quota circumvention.

Exact account duration/cost/balance may be entered manually with source, confirmation time and expiration. Planner invalidates at reset and never refills automatically. Country eligibility and asset licenses remain user checks; app does not issue production permissions.
