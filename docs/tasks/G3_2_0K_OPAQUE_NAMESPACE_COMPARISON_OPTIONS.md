# Opaque namespace comparison options

All candidates remain proposals. Equality of labels has no authority, currentness or authorization implication. Read alongside the original P1 normative contract and the J representation dossier.

| Candidate | Domain and assumptions | Ambiguity and determinism | Security and resources | Migration and PG-C14N-1 | Evidence and owner decisions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| K-CMP-A | Exact octets in an explicitly delimited byte domain | Transport must preserve bytes and distinguish wrapper spelling from payload. Invalid UTF-8 and NUL can be compared as octets while admissibility stays open. Alternate wrappers may decode to one octet sequence. Determinism requires an agreed extraction boundary. | Length and scan work in bytes are distinct from encoded overhead. Display needs safe escaping and redaction to prevent truncation, injection and leakage. | Cross-transport conversion cannot silently carry identity. Canonical PG JSON and bound raw bytes retain separate domains. | Future transport round-trip, binary edge-case and duplicate-wrapper evidence. Decide carrier, decoding boundary and admissibility separately. |
| K-CMP-B | Exact Unicode scalar sequences after strict decoding | Valid UTF-8 and JSON escapes may yield equal scalars, including a supplementary scalar represented by a valid escape pair. Invalid sequences and lone surrogate escapes raise separate representation questions. NFC/NFD remain distinct under this candidate. | Decoder agreement is essential. Controls and visually confusable labels still require display controls. Account for wire bytes, decoded data and scan cost separately. | Existing records acquire no decoded interpretation by default. PG canonical rules govern approved PG artifacts only. | Future decoder parity, escape, control and supplementary-character cases. Decide input encoding, BOM, malformed input and admissibility. |
| K-CMP-C | Separately authorized versioned comparison profile | Each future profile would specify representation, decoding, equality and admissibility. Cross-profile and unknown-profile comparisons would require an explicit decision. Determinism depends on both inputs carrying their approved comparison context. | Profile substitution and downgrade are concerns. Parsing and per-profile work need accounting. | Coexistence would require a future migration decision. No PG version, profile ID or wire format is selected. | Future independent profile and version-isolation evidence. Decide whether versioning is warranted and how profiles would relate. |
| K-CMP-D | Supplied opaque assertions alone | Preserve assertions and differences in provenance. Positive namespace equality and admission remain undecided. | Avoids granting equivalence from display or caller hints. Retention and rendering still have resource and privacy costs. | Historical records remain untouched. No change to PG-C14N-1. | Documentary review can verify withholding of unsupported conclusions. Owner must separately authorize any positive comparison direction. |

**RECOMMENDATION — NOT APPROVED:** K-CMP-D until a comparison boundary and its evidence criteria can be evaluated independently of authority.
