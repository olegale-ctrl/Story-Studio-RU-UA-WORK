## Scope notes that prevent accidental adoption

- [OPEN_CONTRACT_DETAIL] PG contract/id/revision is only a candidate comparison key here. Equal parsed value/canonical binding may come from different raw target wire bytes. I does not choose strict raw artifact freeze or universal byte-conflict policy; H-D03 remains unapproved. [D P1 normative](G3_2_0D_P1_NORMATIVE_CONTRACT.md) P1-05; [H identity analysis](G3_2_0H_IDENTITY_CONFLICT_AND_DEDUP_ANALYSIS.md) PG ObjectRef.

- [PROPOSED] Raw ByteRef has no declared revision slot. A changed expected hash/length cannot be made invisible by treating it as a new stable identity without an explicit future model. This is a conflict-analysis question, not adoption of H-D04 immutable version/admission rules. [H identity analysis](G3_2_0H_IDENTITY_CONFLICT_AND_DEDUP_ANALYSIS.md) Raw ByteRef; [C P2 proposals](G3_2_0C_P2_REFERENCE_AND_SCHEMA.md) §1.

- [OPEN_CONTRACT_DETAIL] Policy semantic role and HeadRef stream/epoch position are not actual grants or latest-state authorities. Storage identity and role-sensitive validation may be distinct comparisons; neither can be inferred from byte sharing. [H identity analysis](G3_2_0H_IDENTITY_CONFLICT_AND_DEDUP_ANALYSIS.md) PolicyRef/HeadRef; [C P2 proposals](G3_2_0C_P2_REFERENCE_AND_SCHEMA.md) §4.

- [APPROVED] SubjectKey binds an exact reviewed subject under X04; it is not the immutable raw registry key. Its intentional component hash fields do not justify putting expected metadata into every object key. New render-affecting plan/tool/profile bindings require a new exact subject/candidate. Concrete policy/version/review continuity remains OPEN. [B owner X01–X04](G3_2_0B_OWNER_DECISIONS_X01_X04.md) X04.

- [APPROVED_LIMITED_SCOPE] Root F-D02/A occurrence accounting says nothing about reference dedup eligibility. F-D03/A report examples do not prove actual zero effects. [PROPOSED] Logical validation reuse, physical bytes/allocations/retries and semantic authority must be separately evidenced, not declared free. [G root accounting](G3_2_0G_ROOT_ACCOUNTING_CONTRACT.md) G-AC-05; [G report boundary](G3_2_0G_NON_AUTHORITY_REPORT_CONTRACT.md) G-RP-01/L01.
