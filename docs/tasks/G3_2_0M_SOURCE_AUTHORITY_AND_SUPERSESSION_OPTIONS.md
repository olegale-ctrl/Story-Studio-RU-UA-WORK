# Source authority and supersession options

Git identifies bytes and ancestry. Authority depends on the owner decision, its scope, and any explicit supersession or revocation. A newer commit may preserve old prose, correct a rendering defect, or amend only a field. Titles, indexes, signatures described only in prose and previously accepted packets establish no new owner decision on their own.

| Option | Provenance and scope | Conflict, partial change and historical status | Missing, stale and cross-project evidence | Audit and maintenance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M-AUTH-A mapping per row family/decision/scope | Pin each source and authority rationale; distinguish proposal and original approval | Preserve duplicates until scoped agreement is shown; supersede only named decision cells | Missing authority or inaccessible source leaves affected cells unresolved; external project scope needs separate evidence | Reproduce row-level map from pinned objects; map maintenance scales with rows |
| M-AUTH-B owner-maintained versioned manifest | Owner provenance for each manifest revision and immutable source references | Explicit partial updates and historical manifest retention; conflicting manifests need owner clarification | Stale manifest or vanished referenced blob blocks correspondence; cross-project entries require explicit delegation | Audit exact manifest bytes and scope history; maintenance includes manifest/source reconciliation |
| M-AUTH-C qualified source chain | Explicit origin, role and link per scoped supersession or revocation | Follow only qualified links for the named field; retain original claims and unresolved divergent chains | Broken links, unknown authority or project transitions interrupt the evidence chain | Independent review requires complete pins and qualification evidence; chain complexity raises maintenance cost |
| M-AUTH-D defer and seek owner resolution | Record complete visible claims and where they came from | Preserve conflicting and duplicate assertions without selecting precedence or reclassifying history | Unavailable, stale or externally scoped sources remain open | Reviewers can replay the evidence inventory; final status depends on separately scoped owner clarification |

A useful comparison is the L packet's historical L-D01 proposal row and the later M owner instruction that authorizes L-D01/A only for documentary research. The latter leaves L-D02/A and L-D03/A unapproved. A FIX-1 replacement of malformed table output is another distinct change, with no new approval. The original and corrected blobs remain independently identifiable. No universal latest-wins rule, authority manifest or supersession procedure is approved.
