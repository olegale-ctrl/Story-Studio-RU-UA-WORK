## D1 — Immutable causal roots vs required-current domain refs

For a HUMAN_EDIT record `Xn`, its exact `H.previous`, `H.input_refs[0]` and its REVISION Run's first `semantic_input_refs[0]` **remain mandatory immutable causal edit-base bindings and fully verified audit ancestry**, but **do not, merely by their presence, become required-current domain refs**. In a currentness check of `Xn`, the previous revision `Xn-1` is a *history carrier*, not a second current version of the same identity. Do not recursively require each predecessor's identity in `observed_active_input_refs`; never discard it from audit Closure or provenance verification.

A HUMAN_EDIT record must still pass `verify_si_provenance` and exact DAG/snapshot validation before declaring CURRENT. A corrupted or missing causal base is **INVALID**, not STALE.

## D2 — Semantic dependency projection for revisions

Determine required-current dependencies for the queried revision as a **typed, role-aware projection** of its **actual current payload** and the domain-specific generating lineage; exclude the REVISION edit-base and metadata edges as such. The `REVISION Run.semantic_input_refs` payload-derived roles must agree with the current payload under the already accepted provenance verifier; they are **not** an instruction to recursively include all historical roots. No implicit latest resolution, no inherited old-version identity requirement and no rewriting of source refs.

A current payload field `F` that explicitly binds the relevant domain role overrides any earlier value of that same role in historical producer metadata: if the revised value differs from the original, **validate the new exact ref as the current dependency**; do not silently use the old ref, and do not silently substitute a newer ref from the observed set. Old bindings stay audit-visible. A still-relevant inherited producer dependency with no current payload replacement keeps its original **exact** SIRef. If the new payload and inherited producer constraints conflict in a way not defined by R1/R1a or cannot be validated, fail closed and return INVALID or BLOCKED_CONTRACT as appropriate; do not claim CURRENT by dropping the constraint.

This is a projection for read-only currentness, **not** a change to RunRecord.semantic_input_refs, H.input_refs, the record wire schema, CreativeAlternative original CREATIVE run pointer, or a new edit operation.

## D3 — CreativeAlternative original CREATIVE Run inheritance

For a CreativeAlternative `K1` generated by original CREATIVE Run `C`, and its HUMAN_EDIT successors `K2`/`K3`:
- `payload.run_ref=C` stays frozen as exact origin provenance and is **not by itself a required-current domain identity**; its exact record and original semantic inputs remain audit/provenance checked.
- Recover original C's **substantive, exact input bindings** required for Creative under R1 §4.2 (including exact EvaluationReport and its still-relevant context/domain dependencies) as candidate inherited currentness dependencies. Do **not** follow C's `input_snapshot` or its `context_snapshot` as currentness requirements.
- Current revision's explicitly typed payload bindings, e.g. `angle_ref` and `hook_claim_refs`, govern corresponding roles and override older producer refs **by role**, not merely by matching exact identity. Keep source/claim/check/evaluation context bindings from C when still applicable; do not require historical K1/K2 or older Angle revisions solely because they are in immutable ancestry. `EvaluationReport` and other immutable C references that remain genuinely required must be present at their original exact revisions in observed active inputs, or the Creative is STALE.
- A changed Angle may have different idea/audience/claim references. Those follow the newly referenced Angle and other explicitly current fields, but **do not automatically replace** original C's EvaluationReport or ChannelContext. If updated Angle is incompatible with still-frozen exact evaluation/channel constraints and no approved regeneration/re-evaluation has occurred, the Creative must **not** become CURRENT: represent valid but obsolete substantive binding as STALE, or invalidate genuinely contradictory declared refs according to existing R1 codes. No implicit `E2` or new channel injection from observed; create a new Creative via its approved operation when original generation dependencies no longer fit.
- Preserve all K1/K2/K3 exact ancestry and original source qualifications. Text-only edits with unchanged substantive dependencies do not make current revision STALE merely because K1/K2 are no longer active.

## D4 — Other domain types and selection/ERef boundaries

Apply D1–D2 to REVISE_INPUT revisions of ordinary SourceItem, StoryIdea, ClaimItem, EvidenceItem, ClaimCheck, AudienceHypothesis, ChannelContext, Angle, Rubric, Risk, VisualRequirement, ProductionBrief and other R1a RevisableRecordSet members, respecting per-type substantive payload refs. An edited SourceItem with no current payload SI dependencies can be CURRENT relative to a valid observed set, despite S1/S2 only in audit ancestry.

When an assembled ProductionBrief has an explicitly used Creative Selection, compare the exact `creative_selection_ref` against observed selection heads; unrelated heads do not stale other domain records. Unrelated heads or selection changes do not stale EvaluationReport or CreativeAlternative. Packet currentness remains separate and checks full frozen context. Any relevant ERef without a supplied exact validated editorial snapshot remains INVALID per approved R1 §1.2; this proposal does not provide or bypass that adapter.

No approval/readiness, actual model execution, semantic truth, rights, storage, UI ReviewState, request replay or producer authority is inferred.
